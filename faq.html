Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining our community.

What are your opening hours?
We are open 24/7 for members with keycard access. Staffed hours are from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM on weekdays, and 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on weekends.

Can I bring a guest?
Yes! Premium members can bring one guest per visit for free. Standard members can bring a guest for a day pass fee of $15.

Do you offer personal training?
Absolutely. We have a team of certified personal trainers specializing in various fields from bodybuilding to rehabilitation. You can book a consultation via the Coaches page.

Is there a contract or cancellation fee?
We offer both month-to-month no-contract plans and discounted 12-month commitments. Cancellation requires a 30-day notice.

Do you have parking?
Yes, we have a large dedicated parking lot for members located at the rear of the building, free of charge.
